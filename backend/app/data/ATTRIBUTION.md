- Gagné et al. 2026, *SPIFF: SPHEREx spectral typing of ultracool dwarfs*, arXiv:2604.22012
- Data: doi:10.5281/zenodo.19051216 (files `spiff_templates_hybrid_csv.zip` and `spiff_tables.zip`)
- License: CC-BY 4.0
